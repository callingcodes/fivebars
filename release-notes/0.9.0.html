First public beta of Fivebars.

  • Your connection quality in the menu bar: five signal bars and the latest ping time.
  • A popover with a 5-minute latency chart and session and all-time statistics.
  • Optional notifications when the connection goes down and comes back.
  • Recovers on its own after sleep, network changes and DNS failures.
  • Unprivileged ICMP over IPv4 and IPv6: no root, no helper.
  • Daily update check through Sparkle; you are asked before anything is installed.
